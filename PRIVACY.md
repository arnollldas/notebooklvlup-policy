## 2. Overview

NotebookLVLUP is a Chrome extension that works entirely on your device. There is **no external server**, **no user account**, and **no registration required**. All stored data remains locally in your browser.

---

## 3. What data is stored?

### 3.1 Local browser data (`chrome.storage.local`)

The following data is stored exclusively in your local browser:

- **Captured links** (Capture Mode): URLs you manually mark using middle-click or Ctrl+Click
- **Folder structure**: Name, color, emoji, and contained notebooks of your NotebookLM folders
- **Settings**: Theme preference (light/dark), onboarding status

This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to any external server.

### 3.2 Access to web pages

- **NotebookLM (notebooklm.google.com)**: The extension reads your notebook structure and injects UI elements (folders, PDF export button). No NotebookLM content is stored or transmitted.
- **All other websites**: Only when Capture Mode is active, the extension highlights links on hover. No page content is read or stored.

---

## 4. Third-party sharing

**No data is shared with third parties.** No analytics tools, tracking services, or external APIs are used.

---

## 5. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

- **Access** your stored data
- **Delete** all stored data

To delete all locally stored data: Chrome → Settings → Extensions → NotebookLVLUP → Clear data. Alternatively, you can uninstall the extension.
